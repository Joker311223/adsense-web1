Party Overview

Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 features a flexible party system where you choose three of the available characters for each combat encounter. Understanding each character's strengths and the synergies between them is critical for late-game success.

Your active party can be swapped freely between battles. Different bosses favor different compositions — don't get attached to a single setup.

Gustave

⚔️ Leader / Physical DPS

The heart of Expedition 33. Gustave excels in sustained physical damage and crowd control, making him the reliable backbone of almost any party composition.

Best Build — "Blade Tempest" (Recommended)

Focuses on maximizing physical damage output through stacking critical hit chance and multi-hit abilities.

  • Primary Skills: Ironclad Cleave, Surge Strike, Blade Tempest (ultimate)
  • Passive: Combat Flow (each parry increases next attack damage by 15%)
  • Equipment: Painted Warblade + Expedition Plate + Counterattacker's Ring
  • Stat Priority: ATK → CRIT → SPD

Best Build — "Guardian Wall" (Tanky)

Sacrifice some damage output for party protection. Excellent for beginners and hard bosses.

  • Primary Skills: Iron Fortress, Rallying Cry, Provoke
  • Passive: Enduring Heart (parry recovers 5% HP)
  • Equipment: Bulwark Shield + Ironclad Armor + Guardian's Talisman
  • Stat Priority: DEF → HP → ATK
💡 Gustave Tip

Gustave's parry counterattacks are surprisingly powerful. Build the "Blade Tempest" spec and intentionally parry regularly — each successful parry stacks Combat Flow, potentially tripling your damage output during a burst turn.

Best Party Compositions

There's no single "best" party for all situations, but these three compositions cover the most ground:

🏆 All-Around Best: Gustave + Maelle + Lune

The classic balanced trio. Gustave deals physical damage and tanks hits, Maelle provides elemental DPS and barriers, and Lune keeps everyone alive. This party can handle 90% of the game's content.

⚡ Speed Nuke: Maelle + Verso + Lune

The fastest party in the game. Maelle weakens enemies with elemental damage, Verso exploits those weaknesses for massive crits, and Lune buffs the team's speed. One of the best setups for the final boss.

🛡️ Hard Mode / Beginners: Gustave + Verso + Lune

Gustave tanks and sustains, Verso bursts, and Lune heals. This party is slower but very forgiving of mistakes — recommended if you're new to the parry system.
