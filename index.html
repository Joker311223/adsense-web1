About the Game

What is Expedition 33?

Developed by Sandfall Interactive and published by Kepler Interactive, Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 is a visually stunning turn-based RPG inspired by French Belle Époque aesthetics. Every year, a mysterious being called the Paintress awakens and erases people from existence — this is the story of those who refuse to accept their fate.

🎨

Stunning Artistry

Hand-crafted watercolor environments blending dream and reality in a world unlike any other RPG.

⚔️

Dynamic Combat

Turn-based strategy meets real-time action — dodge, parry, and counter attacks in a deeply satisfying system.

📖

Epic Narrative

A heartbreaking, emotionally charged story about sacrifice, memory, and the will to survive at the end of the world.

🌍

Rich World

Explore surreal painted landscapes, uncover hidden lore, and encounter unforgettable characters on your journey.

Pro Tips

Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

Clair Obscur punishes unpreparedness. These are the top mistakes new players make — and how to avoid them.

  • 01
    Never skip the parry tutorial

    The parry system is central to every boss fight. Master the timing early or suffer later.

  • 02
    Explore every area thoroughly

    Many of the best weapons and lore items are hidden in easy-to-miss corners of each region.

  • 03
    Manage your party composition

    Different bosses require different setups. Keep all characters leveled, not just your favorites.

  • 04
    Don't sell unique items

    Some items marked as "junk" are actually required for quests or late-game upgrades.
